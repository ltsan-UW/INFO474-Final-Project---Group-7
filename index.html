How does international player performance compare to domestic?
Each circle represents a player, where the size of a circle is the player's VORP.

VORP is a statistic that shows how well a player performs in comparison to an average hypothetical player of the same caliber. This gives us a tool to see how well a player performs overall.

As a result, there are more above average domestic players than international.

And, there are more high performance international players than domestic.

The Growth of International Players in the NBA
Since 1996, the population of non-US born players in the NBA has grown from about 5% to 23%, a significant change in 27 years. Assuming this trend continues, we could well be past the 25% mark within the next few years. As more NBA teams become interested in foreign talent, and as those international players continue to succeed, the data shows that this upward trend is showing no signs of slowing.
