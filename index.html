The NBA's International Players
Basketball is a global game, and the NBA is the biggest basketball league in the world. It's no longer rare to see star players and championship winners come from places other than the United States. But what does the data say?

In this project, we'll explore some facts and data that highlight why fans, coaches, and players alike think the NBA is becoming more global.
The NBA is "becoming more international"
In the modern day, a term that often gets tossed around is that the NBA is becoming "more international". This comes from the current trend of increased relevance and representation of players from outside the United States, where the NBA is based.

The Growth of International Players in the NBA
Since 1996, the population of non-US born players in the NBA has grown from about 5% to 23%, a significant change in 27 years. Assuming this trend continues, we could well be past the 25% mark within the next few years. As more NBA teams become interested in foreign talent, and as those international players continue to succeed, the data shows that this upward trend is showing no signs of slowing.
Highlights from International Players over the Years
This history of non-US born players over the past 25+ years has been littered with awards, championships, and overall some of the greatest basketball players of all time. The magnitude of success has only grown as more and more of the NBA's greatest talent comes from outside the USA. When we think of historically significant NBA players, we think of Americans like Jordan, LeBron, Kobe, or Kareem. But in a few years, we'll also remember some of 2024's international greats, like Jokic, Giannis, Wembenyama, or Shai.
Conclusion
Throughout this project, we've shown that the international community is everywhere in the NBA. From top 10 players, to almost a quarter of the NBA's population, full of award winners, future hall of famers, and NBA champions.

Recent history in the NBA has seen it go from being largely dominated by US born players, to now considering many international players as some of the league's best.
