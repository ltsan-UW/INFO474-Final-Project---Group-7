The Growth of International Players in the NBA
Since 1996, the population of non-US born players in the NBA has grown from about 5% to 23%, a significant change in 27 years. Assuming this trend continues, we could well be past the 25% mark within the next few years. As more NBA teams become interested in foreign talent, and as those international players continue to succeed, the data shows that this upward trend is showing no signs of slowing.
